### 7.1 The forward model (`robust/rt/`, 12 modules, 8,300 lines)

Public entry point `forward(iops, phase_params, geometry, wave, mode,
inelastic=, corrections=)`, JAX, jit/vmap/grad-safe.  Milestones M0–M4
elastic (2026-07-31 to 08-07), M0–M4 inelastic (08-20 to 08-27), M5 CDOM
fluorescence (08-29/30), docs D1/D2 (08-29 to 09-01).

Training data: Loisel et al. 2023 HydroLight, 3,320 water bodies × 81
wavelengths (350–750 nm) × 3 solar zeniths (0/30/60 degrees) = 9,960 samples,
nadir view, one Fournier-Forand phase function, B_p in [0.0103, 0.0180].
Scenario files X=1 (elastic), X=2 (+Raman), X=4 (+Raman +Chl fluorescence at
phi_C = 0.02).  Splits: 20% held-out by scene; zenith hold-out (train 0/30,
test 60).  Weights are tracked and shipped: `emulator_l23.npz` (6.7 KB),
`raman_corr_l23.npz`, `fl_corr_l23.npz`, `ed_l23.npz`.

**Elastic** (`reports/report_rt_elastic_model.md` v1.0, 2026-08-15): hybrid
`rrs = rrs_ZTT (1 + delta)` with a 417-parameter MLP.  Held-out rRMS:

| model | held-out scenes | held-out at 60 deg | unseen zenith (train 0/30, test 60) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gordon 1988 | 7.21% | 9.01% | 9.01% |
| ZTT backbone | 5.93% | 8.11% | 8.09% |
| O25 form refit on L23 | 0.69% | 0.71% | 4.63% |
| hybrid MLP | 0.30% | 0.32% | 4.74–12.24% (5 seeds, median 7.75) |

2.3× better than the O25 refit, 24× better than Gordon, uniform 0.08–0.64%
per wavelength; gradients agree with finite differences to 5e-9.  On the
unseen-zenith split the O25 benchmark wins; this is reported, not gated.
B_p span in L23 is only 1.7×, so phase-function generalisation is untested.

**Inelastic** (`report_rt_inelastic_model.md` v1.0, 2026-08-27): Raman

fluorescence ported term-for-term from the fixed BING physics (cross-check

4.29/1.94/2.18%, corrected 0.34/0.34/0.34% at 0/30/60 degrees.  Truth
magnitudes: Raman 5–15% of Rrs over 520–750 nm, fluorescence about 35% of Rrs
